# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
JOREK community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity.

## Our standards
Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal or political attacks, and
publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers at
{{CONDUCT_CONTACT_EMAIL}}. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.
